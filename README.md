🏁 NISSAN PATROL: OMANI DESERT SAFARI — Full Description
🇴🇲 The Complete Story
Nissan Patrol: Omani Desert Safari is a complete, browser-based desert racing adventure that celebrates the Sultanate of Oman — its golden dunes, its Bedouin heritage, its legendary 4WD culture, and the unmistakable spirit of the Nissan Patrol. Built as a single, self-contained HTML file that runs instantly in any modern browser on desktop or mobile, this game requires no installation, no download, no plugins, and no internet connection after loading. Just open the file and go.

You are behind the wheel of a Nissan Patrol — the undisputed king of the desert — and ahead of you stretches the endless golden expanse of Wahiba Sands and the legendary Empty Quarter (Rub' al Khali). The sun hangs low over Oman. The dunes roll like ocean waves. And somewhere out there, beyond the camel crossings and the sandstorms and the Big Red Dune, lies the hidden treasure and the Bedouin Camp that marks the finish of your desert safari.

"عيش المغامرة العُمانية! تحدَّ الرمال مع نيسان باترول!"
Live the Omani adventure! Conquer the sand with Nissan Patrol!

🎮 What You Actually Do
This is not a passive experience. Every second you're making decisions that matter:

Accelerate across the dunes, building speed and momentum

Brake before rocks and camels or lose control

Jump over obstacles and gaps in the terrain

Turbo boost for bursts of raw 4WD power — but it burns fuel fast

Collect Omani khajoor (dates), fuel cans, turbo pickups, and hidden treasure

Avoid camel crossings and rock formations that will wreck your run

Survive sudden sandstorms that descend without warning

Manage your fuel level, turbo meter, and speed — all simultaneously

Miss a pickup? Lose points. Hit a camel? Lose speed, fuel, and score. Run out of fuel? Your safari ends. Reach 3,000 metres? You complete the desert safari and claim the title of بطل الصحراء — Desert Hero.

The tension is constant: do you push the turbo to grab that treasure chest just ahead, or play it safe and conserve fuel for the long stretch of dunes coming up? Every run is a fresh gamble.

Each mission has a target — 500m, 15 dates, 12 camels avoided, 1 treasure found, 3,000m completed, and so on. As you hit each target, a toast notification pops up in both Arabic and English, a chime plays, and the mission is permanently marked ✓ complete. Complete all eight and you earn the ultimate badge: بطل الصحراء — Desert Hero.

Mission progress and your personal bests (longest distance, most dates, most camels avoided, most storms survived, treasures found) are saved in your browser's local storage, so your progress survives refreshes and restarts.

🎨 Visual & Audio Craft
The presentation is more than skin-deep:

Layered parallax dunes — three distant dune silhouettes move at different speeds to create depth

A setting sun with a radial glow that warms the entire sky

Procedural ground — the terrain is generated mathematically, so every run has fresh hills and dips

Sand ripples across the ground surface for texture

Dust particles kick up from your wheels as you drive and land

Burst effects in distinct colours for each pickup (gold for treasure, cyan for turbo, orange for fuel, green for dates)

Sandstorm overlay with streaking wind lines and a warm fog that reduces visibility

Screen shake on hard landings, camel hits, and rock impacts

Vignette around the edges to focus the eye on the road ahead

Procedural sound — a small Web Audio engine generates beeps and tones for pickups, hits, turbo, and sandstorm warnings (no audio files needed)

Toast notifications that slide in and fade for every achievement

Everything runs on a single <canvas> element with requestAnimationFrame, scaled crisply for high-DPI screens.

💾 Persistence
Three things are saved automatically between sessions using the browser's local storage:

Mission progress — which missions you've completed

Personal bests — your record distance, dates, camels, storms, and treasures

Settings — your chosen car colour and sound preference

A RESET PROGRESS button in Settings lets you wipe everything and start fresh.
